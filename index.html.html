Our Story

Not Just Kebabs.
A System.

Kebab Box was built with one obsessive question: what if every fried chicken kebab tasted exactly the same? Not sometimes. Not on a good day. Every. Single. Time.

Most QSRs rely on individual skill — the chef who's been there 10 years, the one who knows the right oil temperature by feel. We built a brand that removes that dependency entirely.

Standardised recipes. Engineered kitchens. Measurable outcomes. The result? A kebab that tastes identical at outlet one as it will at outlet fifty.
